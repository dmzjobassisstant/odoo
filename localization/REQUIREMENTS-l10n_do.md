### Description
Localization Module for Dominican Republic compatible with NIIF and aligned to DGII regulations.

Includes:
- Standard Chart of Accounts (aligned to DGII and NIIF)
- Preconfigured taxes: ITBIS, ITBIS withholdings, ISR withholdings, tax groups
- Preconfigured sequences for all NCF (Fiscal Documents)
- Fiscal positions for automation of taxes and withholdings
