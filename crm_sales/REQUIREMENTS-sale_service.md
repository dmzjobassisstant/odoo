# sale_service Requirements

## Module Overview
- **Name**: Sales - Service
- **Category**: Sales/Sales
- **Summary**: Interaction between Sales and services apps (project and planning)
- **Description**: Allows to display sale information in the SOL services apps. Additional information is displayed in the name of the SOL when it is used in services apps (project and planning).
- **Author**: Odoo S.A.
- **License**: LGPL-3

## Technical Details

### Dependencies
- `sale_management`

### Purpose
Displays sale information in service apps (project and planning). Shows additional information in sale order line names when used in services apps.
